# Welcome to Acme Robotics

This is the Acme Robotics team handbook. It collects the things a new hire needs
in their first week: how we work, who to ask, and where the important documents live.

## How to use this handbook

- Start with [Onboarding](onboarding.md) on your first day.
- The [Engineering Guide](../engineering/guide.md) covers how we ship software.
- People-related policies (time off, remote work) live under `people/`.

If something here is out of date, open a pull request — the handbook is owned by
everyone on the team, not just the people team.

## Our values

We optimize for **clarity over cleverness**, **small reversible changes**, and
**writing things down** so knowledge does not live only in one person's head.
