# Onboarding checklist

Welcome aboard! This checklist gets you productive in your first week. See the
[handbook welcome](welcome.md) page for the bigger picture.

## Day 1

- Get your laptop and accounts from IT.
- Read the [Engineering Guide](../engineering/guide.md).
- Skim the [Code Review](../engineering/code-review.md) norms before your first PR.

## Week 1

- Pair with your onboarding buddy on a small change.
- Read the [Remote Work](../people/remote-work.md) and [Time Off](../people/time-off.md)
  policies so you know how we operate day to day.
- Introduce yourself in the team channel.

## Questions

If you are stuck, ask early. Nobody expects you to know the codebase in week one,
and asking a clear question is one of our values.
