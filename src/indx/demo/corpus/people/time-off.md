# Time Off Policy

How time off works at Acme Robotics. Part of the people section of the
[handbook](../handbook/welcome.md).

## Vacation

Take the time you need. We expect everyone to take at least three weeks a year. Tell
your team in advance and update the shared calendar.

## Sick leave

If you are unwell, rest. Notify your team async — see the
[Remote Work](remote-work.md) policy for how we communicate while away.

## Holidays

We observe local public holidays based on where you live. The people team publishes
the list at the start of each year.
