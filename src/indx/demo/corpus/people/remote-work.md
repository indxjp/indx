# Remote Work Policy

Acme Robotics is remote-friendly. This policy is part of the people section of the
[handbook](../handbook/welcome.md).

## Working hours

We do not track hours. We track outcomes. Overlap with your team for at least four
hours a day so reviews and pairing stay quick — see [Code Review](../engineering/code-review.md)
for why fast review matters.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a monthly stipend for home-office equipment.
File expenses through the people team.

## Async first

Write decisions down. A short document beats a long meeting, and it can be indexed,
searched, and read by the next person who has the same question.
